Mapping the CDSS to the DAC layers

LayerWhat the DAC layer doesCDSS mapping
Reactive Internal needs with setpoints; the gap from setpoint drives action; competing needs are kept in balance. The patient needs that the weekly choice can move (learning, engagement, coverage, novelty), estimated from their signals and regulated through activity selection.
Adaptive Compress sensory and internal state into a low-dimensional cognitive map; tile the space. Almost a no-op here: the state is already compact and engineered: deficit vector, activity profiles, and recent ΔDM and adherence. Little extra compression needed.
Contextual Store state–action–reward episodes; retrieve them by similarity and reward. Per-patient memory of each activity’s past ΔDM and adherence; weekly retrieval scores the candidates via Score = adherence + ΔDM + PPF.
Virtualization Simulate or replay outcomes to evaluate strategies before acting; curiosity adds an information-gain reward. Backtesting candidate prescriptions over NEST and AISN, plus the currently-absent exploration / novelty term.
